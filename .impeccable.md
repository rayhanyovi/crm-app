## Design Context

### Users
UnifiedCRM is a portfolio CRM demo for interviewers evaluating fullstack product engineering. The in-app users are sales reps, managers, admins, and read-only viewers working through leads, contacts, companies, deals, activities, notifications, and audit trails.

### Brand Personality
Professional, efficient, and credible. The interface should feel like a compact SaaS operations tool: calm enough for repeated use, polished enough for a portfolio review, and explicit about role-based capabilities.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean light-mode business-tool interface inspired by Linear, Attio, and simplified HubSpot. Prioritize dense tables, compact navigation, restrained color, clear status badges, and fast state transitions. Avoid landing-page treatment, decorative gradients, empty marketing copy, and playful visual noise.

### Design Principles
- Put the working product on the first screen; avoid marketing wrappers.
- Favor scannable density: compact headers, useful metadata, predictable navigation, and restrained cards only where they frame real content.
- Make RBAC visible in the UI through available navigation and actions, while keeping server-side checks authoritative.
- Design empty, loading, and error states as product surfaces, not placeholders.
- Keep the demo fast and reliable: minimal animation, accessible controls, and responsive desktop/tablet-first layout.
